Detailed Description

A wrapper around a concrete socket that serializes / deserializes packets.

Socket abstracts over TCP socket, local socket and WebSocket transports. It exposes packet-based signals so that the upper layers (Server, Client, Agent) can work with QMdmmCore::Packet without caring about the underlying transport.
